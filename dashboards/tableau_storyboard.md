# Tableau Public Storyboard (Business Narrative)

## Page 1: Executive Pulse
- KPI cards: Total cases, total deaths, mean CFR, countries covered
- Trend line: global weekly deaths per million
- Filter panel: country, continent, date range

## Page 2: Vaccination vs Mortality
- Dual-axis trend: vaccination rate vs deaths per million
- Scatter: vaccination rate vs weekly deaths (country snapshots)

## Page 3: Country Benchmarking
- Ranked bar: peak deaths per million by country
- Quadrant segmentation: avg vaccination vs avg deaths
- Tooltips: population, 65+ share, hospital beds

## Page 4: Peer Clusters
- Cluster view (or grouped scatter) using:
  - hospital beds per thousand
  - aged 65+
  - deaths per million
- Narrative text: which country types faced highest burden

## Page 5: Forecast Outlook (AI)
- Actual vs predicted weekly deaths for selected country
- Forecast horizon with uncertainty note
- Story callout: operational planning implication

## Filters to Expose Publicly
- Country multi-select
- Continent
- Date range
- Metric selector (cases, deaths, CFR, vaccination)
